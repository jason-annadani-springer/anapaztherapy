Fees & funding

  • Individual CBT session: £100.
  • Session length: 50-60 minutes.
  • All sessions provided online.
  • Free 30-minute introductory session.
  • Payment: fees are payable directly by the client before each session, unless alternative arrangements have been agreed in advance.
  • Cancellation fee: the full session fee will be charged for missed appointments or appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice.

Private health insurance

If you have private health insurance, your policy may provide some cover for CBT. As policies and requirements vary between insurers, please contact your insurance provider before starting therapy to confirm whether treatment with an independent therapist is covered and whether prior authorisation is required.

If you need urgent support

APT is not a crisis or emergency service. If you are currently in crisis or experiencing thoughts of suicide, please reach out for immediate support from:

The Samaritans, who are available 24/7, free of charge, on 116 123; or

if you are in immediate danger, please call 999 or go to your nearest A&E.
