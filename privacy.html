Privacy policy

Last updated: 11/2026

At APT, your privacy is very important. This policy explains how I collect, use, store, and protect your personal information, in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, as amended. ICO registration number: [Insert registration number] The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) is the UK's independent data protection regulator. For further information, visit ico.org.uk.

  1. Use of my therapy suite app

    I use My Therapy Suite, a secure, UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018-compliant practice management platform, to safely manage my clinic's day-to-day operations and administer your care. This includes processing and storing your personal information, such as appointment bookings, health records, clinical notes, billing data, secure communications, and video consultations. The platform utilizes advanced encryption and secure servers to protect your data; for more detailed information on how your data is handled, please visit the My Therapy Suite Privacy Policy.

  2. Information I collect

    When you book an appointment, make an enquiry, or receive therapy services with me online, I collect only the personal information necessary to respond to your enquiry, manage appointments, and provide safe, effective, and ethical care. This may include your name, contact details, date of birth, GP contact details, health history, presenting concerns, completed intake forms, therapy session notes, appointment history, and billing and payment information. I may also collect any other information you choose to share with me in the course of our therapeutic work. Health information is classified as special category personal data under UK data protection law and receives additional protection.

  3. How I collect your information

    I generally collect information directly from you through enquiries, booking forms, intake forms, emails, phone calls and therapy sessions. Where relevant and appropriate, I may also receive information from a healthcare professional or another source where this is agreed with you or otherwise lawful. I aim to collect only information that is relevant and necessary for the purposes explained in this policy.

  4. How I use your information and the legal basis for processing

    I use your personal information to respond to enquiries, manage appointments, provide therapy, maintain clinical records, process payments, communicate with you and meet my legal, professional, safeguarding and insurance obligations. Where necessary and lawful, I may also use information to address concerns about safety. Under UK data protection law, I must have a lawful basis for processing personal information and an additional legal condition when processing health information. I will handle your information only for appropriate purposes and in accordance with my legal and professional responsibilities.

  5. How your information is stored and protected

    I take reasonable steps to ensure that access to your information is limited to what is necessary and that information is handled securely. Your records are stored securely through My Therapy Suite, which uses encryption and complies with UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). Any physical records (if any) are kept in locked, restricted.

  6. Record retention

    I retain client records in accordance with applicable legal requirements, professional guidance and relevant insurance obligations. My intended standard retention period for client records is seven years. This period must be appropriate to the type of record and the circumstances of the individual case.

    In some circumstances, records may need to be retained for longer where there is a lawful and justified reason to do so, including relevant legal, professional, safeguarding or insurance requirements. When records are no longer required, they will be securely deleted, destroyed or anonymised, as appropriate.

  7. Online therapy

    APT offers online therapy through My Therapy Suite's integrated video platform or other appropriate online services. Online therapy sessions are not recorded by me. I use the security measures available through the relevant platform to help protect the confidentiality of sessions and related communications.

    To support your privacy, I encourage you to attend online appointments from a quiet, private location where you are unlikely to be overheard or interrupted. You may also wish to use headphones and ensure that your device and internet connection are secure.

    For safety and risk management purposes, I will establish appropriate emergency contact arrangements at the initial assessment. At the beginning of each online session, I may also confirm your current location to ensure that appropriate support can be accessed if an emergency arises. Please let me know if you are attending a session from a different location.

    By booking an online appointment, you acknowledge that therapy will be delivered remotely and that, as with any online service, there may be privacy and security limitations.

  8. Email and electronic communication

    I may use email through My Therapy Suite or other appropriate communication methods to confirm appointments, share relevant resources and respond to general administrative enquiries. Email is not recommended for discussing sensitive clinical information and is not used to conduct therapy sessions.

    My Therapy Suite’s Secure Messaging feature may be used to communicate with me through the platform. You may opt out of email communication at any time. If you prefer another method of communication, please let me know so that we can discuss appropriate alternatives.

  9. Use of artificial intelligence (AI)

    To help me stay fully present during our sessions and maintain accurate, timely clinical records, I may use secure, AI-assisted transcription software provided through my practice management platform, My-Therapy-Suite.

    How it works and how your privacy is protected:

    • Your Consent Comes First: Using AI transcription is entirely optional. I will always ask for your explicit, informed consent before using this tool, and you can choose to opt out or withdraw your consent at any time without affecting your therapy.

    • Supporting Clinical Care: The software converts session audio into a draft summary (such as a structured CBT session note). It is used strictly as an administrative aid - I personally review, edit, and approve every clinical note to ensure it is accurate. AI is never used to make clinical decisions or provide therapy.

    • Strict Data Security: All session data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and hosted securely on servers within the United Kingdom in compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

    • No AI Training on Your Data: Your sessions remain strictly confidential. Under formal Data Processing Agreements, neither My-Therapy-Suite nor its contracted AI sub-processors (Anthropic and OpenAI) store your recordings permanently or use your personal information to train public AI models.

    • Automatic Deletion: Once I have reviewed and finalised your written session note, the raw audio recording and unedited transcript are permanently deleted.

    If you have any questions about how your data is handled or would prefer that I take notes manually, please feel free to let me know at any point.

    The aim is not to replace the therapist’s clinical judgement, but to reduce administrative tasks so they can focus more on what really matters: supporting you throughout your therapeutic journey.

    You can find out more at https://mytherapysuite.com/Articles/ai-note-taking-for-therapists

  10. Your rights under data protection law

    Under applicable data protection legislation, you may have the right to:

    • Access your information: Request a copy of the personal information I hold about you.
    • Request corrections: Ask me to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
    • Request erasure: Ask for your personal information to be deleted in certain circumstances.
    • Restrict processing: Request that the use of your information be restricted in certain circumstances.
    • Object to processing: Object to certain uses of your information where the relevant legal conditions apply.
    • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on your consent, withdraw it at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

    These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. For example, I may need to retain some information to meet legal, professional or insurance obligations, even if you request its deletion. If you wish to exercise your rights, please contact me in writing. I will respond in accordance with applicable data protection legislation and explain any relevant limitations where appropriate.

    You can also find information about your data protection rights through the Information Commissioner's Office.

  11. Contact and privacy enquiries

    If you have questions about this policy, would like to request access to your records, or have concerns about how your personal information is handled, please contact me through My Therapy Suite or using the contact details provided by APT. Contact details: info@anapaztherapy.co.uk or My Therapy Suite booking/contact page link]. Please avoid including sensitive clinical information in general email enquiries.

  12. Updates to this policy

    APT may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in practice, technology, professional guidance or legal requirements. The latest version will be made available on the APT website.
