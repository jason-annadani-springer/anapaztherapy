About me

Ana Paz, MSc CBT | BABCP Accredited CBT Therapist | DipSW

I am a British-Spanish CBT Therapist with a Master’s degree in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy from Buckinghamshire New University, England, and a Diploma in Social Work from the University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain. I am an accredited member of the British Association for Behavioural and Cognitive Psychotherapies (BABCP, Member #). I offer personalised CBT for adults, with a particular focus on trauma and anxiety, while also supporting a wide range of other challenges that may be affecting your wellbeing

If you need urgent support

APT is not a crisis or emergency service. If you’re currently in crisis or experiencing thoughts of suicide, please reach out for immediate support.

Samaritans are available 24/7, free of charge, on 116 123.

If you are in immediate danger, please call 999 or go to your nearest A&E.
